## New Research Framework Added

## Figure 1: Responsible AI-Assisted Early Pediatric Vision Risk Detection Before Age 3

## A conceptual hospital-integrated workflow framework exploring how responsible AI could support:

Early pediatric vision risk identification
EHR-connected risk signals
Clinician review and oversight
Referral support pathways
Pediatric ophthalmology evaluation
Follow-up tracking and outcome validation

## The framework emphasizes responsible AI principles, including:

Human oversight
Privacy and security
Bias monitoring
Explainability
Non-diagnostic AI outputs

## Research Goal

Identify children at risk for amblyopia, strabismus, or abnormal visual development before avoidable delays in referral occur.

This framework reflects ongoing independent research into healthcare AI deployment, pediatric vision screening, and real-world implementation challenges.
