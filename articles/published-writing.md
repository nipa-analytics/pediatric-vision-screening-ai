# Before Age 3: Why Pediatric Vision AI Should Start Inside Pediatric Healthcare Systems

https://medium.com/@nipashah2311/before-age-3-why-pediatric-vision-ai-should-start-inside-pediatric-healthcare-systems-0d51515d80e6

## Brief Description

This article explores why responsible AI-assisted pediatric vision screening should begin earlier inside hospital and pediatric healthcare systems, especially before age 3. The focus is on early risk detection for amblyopia, strabismus, and abnormal visual development using pediatric workflows, EHR-connected signals, clinician review, and referral pathways.

The article argues that pediatric vision AI should not be designed as a standalone diagnostic tool or consumer app. Instead, it should support healthcare teams by helping identify risk signals earlier, track referral gaps, and connect children to licensed eye-care professionals before avoidable delays occur.

## Key Focus Areas

* Early pediatric vision risk detection before age 3
* Amblyopia and strabismus referral support
* Hospital and pediatric clinic workflow integration
* EHR-based longitudinal risk signals
* Responsible AI safeguards
* Clinician-reviewed, non-diagnostic AI support
* Real-world validation for very young children
* Referral and follow-up tracking

## Research Relevance

This article supports the broader goal of this repository: exploring how responsible AI can assist early pediatric vision screening while maintaining safety, fairness, explainability, privacy, and clinical accountability.
